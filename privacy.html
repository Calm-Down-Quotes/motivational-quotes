Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026. The site is designed to work with minimal personal data.

1. Information you provide

The core website does not require an account, name, email address or login. If a separate newsletter, contact method or external service is linked from the site, information you choose to provide to that service is handled under that provider's privacy terms.

2. Local browser storage

The quote engine uses browser storage on your device to remember settings such as your chosen atmosphere, saved quotes, quote sequence, selected focus and action-streak dates. This information is stored locally in your browser and is not intentionally transmitted to us by the quote engine.

You can remove this information by clearing site data in your browser. The site will continue to work, but saved settings and quotes will be reset.

3. Analytics and hosting

The site may use privacy-focused traffic measurement provided by its hosting or analytics provider, including Cloudflare Web Analytics where enabled. Such tools can process technical information needed to measure visits and performance, such as page views, device or browser characteristics, approximate geographic information and network information.

We use aggregated traffic information to understand whether the site works correctly, which pages are useful and how performance can be improved. We do not intentionally use the quote engine to build personal profiles.

4. Advertising and affiliate links

At the date of this policy, the rebuilt site does not intentionally load personalised advertising in the core experience. Advertising, sponsorships or affiliate links may be introduced in the future. If they are introduced, relevant disclosures and this policy will be updated where necessary, and third-party providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. External links

The website may link to third-party websites or services. We do not control their content, security or privacy practices. Review their policies before providing personal information.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to a static public website, including restrictive browser security policies where supported. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The site is general motivational content and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website changes, particularly if new analytics, advertising, subscriptions or paid services are introduced. The date at the top identifies the current version.

9. Questions

Where a contact channel is published on the site or through an associated subscription service, it may be used for privacy questions or correction requests.
